# Code of Conduct

NOVA follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.
In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, and keep criticism about the work, never the person. Harassment, insults, unwelcome
attention and publishing someone's private information are not acceptable here.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately at the email address
on the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/abhiramchendika-svg). Reports are kept
confidential. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block someone from the project,
following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
